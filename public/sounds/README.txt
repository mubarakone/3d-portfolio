SOUND EFFECTS INSTRUCTIONS

Place the following sound files in this directory:

# UI Interaction Sounds
1. ui-click.mp3 - Sound that plays when clicking menu items
   [Recommended: Click or tap sound, very short 0.1-0.3 second duration]

2. hover.mp3 - Sound that plays when hovering over menu items
   [Recommended: Very subtle soft tick or hover sound, very short 0.05-0.2 second duration and low volume]

3. progress-loading.mp3 - Sound that plays during progress bar loading
   [Recommended: Subtle tick, beep or loading sound, very short 0.1-0.3 second duration as it plays multiple times]

4. enter-button.mp3 - Sound that plays when clicking the Enter button
   [Recommended: Distinctive click or activation sound, 0.2-0.5 second duration]

Tips for good sound effects:
- Keep sound durations short for better user experience
- Ensure volume levels are consistent across all sounds
- Use MP3 format for better browser compatibility
- For hover sounds, use very subtle and short effects to avoid annoyance
- For the progress loading sound, use a short sound that works well when played repeatedly

You can use any sound files you prefer, just make sure they are named correctly.
